# Problem: Smallest Divisible Digit Product I

## Description
You are given two integers `n` and `t`.  
Return the smallest number greater than or equal to `n` such that the product of its digits is divisible by `t`.

---

## Examples

### Example 1
**Input:**  
n = 10, t = 2

**Output:**  
10

**Explanation:**  
Digit product of 10 = 0, which is divisible by 2.  
Hence, 10 is the smallest number ≥ 10 that satisfies the condition.

---

### Example 2
**Input:**  
n = 15, t = 3

**Output:**  
16

**Explanation:**  
Digit product of 16 = 6, which is divisible by 3.  
Hence, 16 is the smallest number ≥ 15 that satisfies the condition.

---

## Constraints
- \(1 \leq n \leq 100\)  
- \(1 \leq t \leq 10\)
