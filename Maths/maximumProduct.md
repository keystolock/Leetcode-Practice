# 628. Maximum Product of Three Numbers

**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Topics:** Greedy, Sorting, Math  
**Companies:** premium lock icon  

---

## Problem Statement
Given an integer array `nums`, find three numbers whose product is maximum and return the maximum product.

---

## Examples

**Example 1:**
Input: nums = [1,2,3]
Output: 6

**Example 2:**
Input: nums = [1,2,3,4]
Output: 24

**Example 3:**
Input: nums = [-1,-2,-3]
Output: -6

---

## Constraints
- \(3 \leq nums.length \leq 10^4\)  
- \(-1000 \leq nums[i] \leq 1000\)
